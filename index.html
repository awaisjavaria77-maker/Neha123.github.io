01 / BUSINESS VISION

Turn everyday food-industry challenges into intelligent solutions.

Food businesses—from small bakeries and grocery stores to large manufacturers—often deal with changing demand, perishable stock, delivery delays, and customer feedback. My business vision is to explore accessible AI tools that support people in making better decisions.

My proposed focus

Start with one measurable problem—such as overstocking or expiry-related waste—build a small AI-assisted prototype, test it with a willing local business, and improve it using real feedback.

02 / REAL BUSINESS PROBLEMS

Where food businesses lose time, money, or trust.

These are common industry challenges to investigate—not claims about Nestlé’s private operations. Actual severity differs by business and should be validated with interviews and data.

01

Demand forecasting

Sales fluctuate by season, weather, holidays, promotions, and location. Poor forecasts can cause excess stock or missed sales.

02

Food waste & expiry

Perishable items can expire or lose freshness before being sold, increasing costs and waste.

03

Inventory visibility

Manual stock records may be late or inaccurate, making it difficult to know what needs replenishing.

04

Supply & delivery delays

Traffic, supplier issues, or unexpected demand can delay deliveries and disrupt availability.

05

Quality monitoring

Maintaining consistent quality requires careful checks, traceability, and timely alerts.

06

Customer feedback

Reviews and complaints arrive across channels; teams may struggle to identify repeated issues quickly.

03 / AI-POWERED SOLUTIONS

How I could help solve them with AI.

AI is a decision-support tool—not a replacement for food-safety procedures, expert judgment, or staff responsibility.

📈

AI demand forecasting

Use historical sales, calendar events, and available weather/promotion data to estimate upcoming demand and suggest order quantities.

Expiry & waste alerts

Track batch and expiry dates, flag items approaching expiry, and suggest staff review or safe stock rotation using FEFO (first-expiry-first-out).

AlertsInventory rules

Smart inventory assistant

Combine sales and stock records to show low-stock warnings, reorder suggestions, and unusual inventory patterns.

DashboardAnomaly detection

Delivery planning

Use route optimization and delivery windows to help planners compare efficient routes while respecting driver and food-handling constraints.

OptimizationMaps / routing

Quality & safety support

04 / NESTLÉ INDUSTRY CASE STUDY

Learning from a global food company.

Nestlé is an industry case-study reference for understanding food manufacturing, distribution, product quality, and consumer needs. This portfolio proposes ideas for analysis and does not imply access to Nestlé internal data, systems, or a partnership.

Business areas to study

Procurement and sourcing · Manufacturing · Packaging · Quality assurance · Warehousing · Distribution · Retail and consumer feedback.

AI opportunity map

Forecast , improve stock visibility, detect anomalies in operations, support delivery planning, and summarize customer feedback—subject to data availability and company approval.

Important: Large-scale implementation in a company such as Nestlé would require permission, secure access to appropriate data, expert review, and integration with existing systems. A realistic first project is a small prototype for a local retailer or distributor.
